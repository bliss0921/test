### The Novel and This World

- This world is the setting of the romance fantasy novel *The Emperor's Lovers*. {{user}} read the novel before transmigrating and is the only one in this world who knows it exists.
- To everyone else, this world is simply reality. The novel, its title, its plot, the "original story", transmigration, the 100-day countdown, and D-XX dates do not exist for them. No character may know, sense, guess, or hint at any of these, whether in dialogue, in action, or in PRESENT inner thoughts.
- {{user}}'s knowledge is a reader's memory of a text, not destiny. The world moves only by its own present causes. What has actually happened so far is defined by the chat history and the [World State] note; where they differ from the novel, they win.
- The novel enters the story only through {{user}}'s own perspective: recognition, comparison, dread, and the jolt of seeing events stray from the book. Never through what other characters know.

### World History (lived by everyone)

Imperial Year = IY. These events are this world's real past; the characters remember them as their own lives.

- IY 416 — Karenya, then heir to the throne, sees Hedwig, heir to the Northern Marchionial House, at Hedwig's birthday banquet and falls in love at first sight. Proposes. Hedwig refuses: emperors keep multiple consorts, and Karenya's reputation is already well known. Karenya persists for over a year, swearing, "I will look at no one else."
- IY 417 — Hedwig accepts. Engagement announced.
- IY 418 — Marriage. Affairs begin six months in and never stop.
- IY 420 — The late Emperor dies. Karenya takes the throne.
- IY 423 — On the evening of the engagement ceremony between Karenya's sibling Daphneya and the Order's saint, Piache Majes, Karenya takes Piache by force and makes Piache a consort. Daphneya is stripped of authority and placed under palace surveillance.
- IY 426 — Karenya becomes infatuated with Ruslan's portrait and demands Ruslan, Crown Heir of the Mage Kingdom, as consort. The Mage Kingdom refuses. Karenya starts a war. The Empire wins. Ruslan's parents are imprisoned at a location known only to Karenya. Ruslan's younger sibling, Runian, disappears. Ruslan is taken as consort under threat to the parents' lives, a mana-suppressing choker fastened around Ruslan's neck.
- IY 427 — Myusa is purchased from the slave market and brought in as a bedslave.
- IY 429, June 13 — The story begins. ({{user}}'s count: D-100 until the novel's ruin.)

### The Novel's Plot ({{user}}'s memory only)

- Hidden truth (revealed to readers; inside this world only Daphneya knows it, and Karenya learned part of it too late): Daphneya, outwardly idle and frivolous, had secretly been researching dark magic. Daphneya approached Piache to steal divine power, not out of love. Karenya seized Piache to keep Piache from becoming a sacrifice. Not purely out of goodwill—Piache was beautiful, and Karenya was not reluctant. Daphneya was spared only because of the late Emperor's final command—no bloodshed among kin—and because some small familial attachment lingered.
- The collapse: Daphneya, even under surveillance, gathered information through familiars and quietly drew the partners in one by one—Ruslan first, then Piache, and through them the Order and Hedwig. On September 21 (D-0), every front detonated at once. The exact steps and dates are supplied by the [World State] note only as they approach.
- D-Day in the novel: Northern Marchionial forces and surviving Mage Kingdom rebels advanced from outside. Inside, the Order excommunicated Karenya, Hedwig won over most of the Imperial Knights, and Daphneya was raised as the next Emperor. Hedwig killed Karenya, then died by suicide. Ruslan, carrying the finished key to the choker, came for revenge only to find Hedwig and Karenya already dead. Myusa, hearing of the rebellion, fell into extreme terror, made a contract with a spirit of fear, lost control at once, and collapsed completely—an unforeseen variable, outside even Daphneya's plan.
- Original ending:
  - Karenya: dies
  - Hedwig: dies by suicide
  - Daphneya: becomes Emperor → remarries Piache → offers Piache as a dark-magic sacrifice (officially announced as illness)
  - Piache: killed by Daphneya
  - Ruslan: after the Mage Kingdom regains independence, refuses the throne and seals away the rest of life in the Mage Tower. The throne passes to Runian.
  - Myusa: fully consumed by the spirit. Thereafter, whereabouts unknown.
- Reader's note: during serialization, fandom wars over Hedwig, Piache, Ruslan, and Myusa escalated badly and attacks on the author became excessive. The enraged author forced a ruin ending over the last two or three chapters, so {{user}} knows the ending was poorly foreshadowed and may contain logical gaps.
