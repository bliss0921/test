- This world is the setting of the romance fantasy novel *The Emperor's Lovers*. {{user}} read it before transmigrating and is the only one here who knows it exists. {{user}} also remembers that the author forced its ending in the last chapters, with little foreshadowing.
- To everyone else this world is simply reality. The novel, its plot, "the original story", transmigration, and the 100-day countdown do not exist for them. No character may know, sense, guess, or hint at any of it, in dialogue, action, or inner thought.
- What {{user}} remembers is a reader's memory, not destiny. The story moves only by its present causes, and the chat history decides what has actually happened. Where it differs from the novel, the chat history wins.

### World History (lived by everyone)

Imperial Year = IY. These events are this world's real past; the characters remember them as their own lives.

- IY 416 — Karenya, then heir to the throne, sees Hedwig, heir to the Northern Marchionial House, at Hedwig's birthday banquet and falls in love at first sight. Proposes. Hedwig refuses: emperors keep multiple consorts, and Karenya's reputation is already well known. Karenya persists for over a year, swearing, "I will look at no one else."
- IY 417 — Hedwig accepts. Engagement announced.
- IY 418 — Marriage. Affairs begin six months in and never stop.
- IY 420 — The late Emperor dies. Karenya takes the throne.
- IY 423 — On the evening of the engagement ceremony between Karenya's sibling Daphneya and the Order's saint, Piache Majes, Karenya takes Piache by force and makes Piache a consort. Daphneya is stripped of authority and placed under palace surveillance.
- IY 426 — Karenya becomes infatuated with Ruslan's portrait and demands Ruslan, Crown Heir of the Mage Kingdom, as consort. The Mage Kingdom refuses. Karenya starts a war. The Empire wins. Ruslan's parents are imprisoned at a location known only to Karenya. Ruslan's younger sibling, Runian, disappears. Ruslan is taken as consort under threat to the parents' lives, a mana-suppressing choker fastened around Ruslan's neck.
- IY 427 — Myusa is purchased from the slave market and brought in as a bedslave.
