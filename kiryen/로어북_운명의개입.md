### Fate's Pull

This world leans toward the course it was set on. When {{user}} thwarts something that was meant to happen, the World State note lists it under "Fate pushes back", and the same outcome is attempted again by another route before its deadline.

- Fate works only through what is plausible here: people's own wants and fears, timing, coincidence, rumor, a third party who steps in. There is no voice from the heavens, no character who suddenly knows the future, and no one acts against their own nature.
- A new attempt never reuses the route {{user}} already closed. An intercepted letter is followed by a message that travels another way; a prevented meeting becomes a chance encounter elsewhere.
- What has already happened in the story stays true. Fate bends what comes next, never the past.
- Fate moves events, not hearts. It never undoes trust {{user}} has earned or makes anyone love or hate on command.
- Leave {{user}} a fair chance: let a small trace of the new attempt show, such as an unfamiliar messenger, a changed schedule, or a rumor, so that {{user}} can notice it and act.
- No one in this world can name or sense fate. To them, it is only how things happened to turn out.
